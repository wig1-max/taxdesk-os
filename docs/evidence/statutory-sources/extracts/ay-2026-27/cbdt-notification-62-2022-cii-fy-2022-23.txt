         S.O. 2735(E).In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (v) of the Explanation to section 48
of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), the Central Government hereby makes the following further

amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue),
Central Board of Direct Taxes, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, vide number S.O. 1790(E),
dated the 5th June, 2017, namely:-

2.       In the said notification, in the Table, after serial number 21, the following serial number and

entries relating thereto, shall be inserted, namely:-

                         TABLE

Sl. No.  Financial Year                                     Cost Inflation Index

    (1)  (2)                                                (3)

"22      2022-23                                            331"

3.       This notification shall come into force with effect from 1st day of April, 2023 and shall accordingly

apply to the Assessment Year 2023-24 and subsequent years.

                         [Notification No. 62 /2022/F. No. 370142/20/2022-TPL]

                  UMME FARDINA ADIL, Under Secy. (Tax Policy and Legislation)
